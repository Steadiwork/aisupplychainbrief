2026-11-06 · Electrical gear leads stretch on AI power demand

ProcurementSupply chain for AI

Eaton data-center orders more than double as electrical demand outruns new capacity

More than 100% growth in rolling 12-month data-center orders. That's what Eaton reported on its November 3 earnings call, alongside data-center revenue up about 65% and a ninth straight quarter of data-center growth above 35%. CEO Paulo Ruiz said Electrical Americas backlog is up 33% and "demand continues to run ahead of what we can convert," even as organic sales in that segment rose 22%. Electrical book-to-bill sat at 1.2, with Americas at 1.3, and total electrical backlog above $20 billion, up 46% year over year. The company also announced general price increases for Electrical Americas and Global effective January 2, 2027. Why it matters: AI data-center buildouts are pulling the same switchgear, UPS and distribution gear plants and warehouses buy for everyday work. Materials managers with electrical lines on open POs or 2027 capital plans may be working from lead times and quotes that no longer hold.

Generac's data-center generator backlog hits about $4.5 billion after Amazon deal

About $4.5 billion. That's Generac's data-center generator backlog as of its October 28 results, nearly triple the level from 90 days earlier, after a long-term supply agreement with Amazon for backup power at data centers. Initial deliveries under that deal are expected to total a combined $2.4 billion in 2027 and 2028. C&I segment external sales rose about 28% in the quarter, and management said data-center demand is still adding orders from non-hyperscale customers too.

Luminovo opens electronics BOMs and lead times to ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot

Ask your AI assistant for the EOL parts and 12-week lead times in your active BOMs. On November 5 Luminovo said it is opening its electronics supply-chain platform to assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot through the Model Context Protocol, with a beta for selected customers at electronica in Munich. An EMS cost estimator can price a customer BOM against current contracts; a buyer can ask which active-BOM parts are end-of-life. Luminovo says Omega EMS cut complex BOM quoting from about half a day to about 20 minutes.
