1. Eaton data-center orders more than double as electrical demand outruns new capacity

More than 100% growth in rolling 12-month data-center orders. That's what Eaton reported on its November 3 earnings call, alongside data-center revenue up about 65% and a ninth straight quarter of data-center growth above 35%. CEO Paulo Ruiz said Electrical Americas backlog is up 33% and "demand continues to run ahead of what we can convert," even as organic sales in that segment rose 22%. Electrical book-to-bill sat at 1.2, with Americas at 1.3, and total electrical backlog above $20 billion, up 46% year over year. The company also announced general price increases for Electrical Americas and Global effective January 2, 2027. Why it matters: AI data-center buildouts are pulling the same switchgear, UPS and distribution gear plants and warehouses buy for everyday work. Materials managers with electrical lines on open POs or 2027 capital plans may be working from lead times and quotes that no longer hold.

So what for materials managersRe-check lead times and quote validity on electrical gear this week, and escalate any line that slipped more than two weeks.

What to do next
  1. Export open POs and planned buys for switchgear, UPS, breakers, transformers and related electrical gear, with supplier, qty, promise date and quote date.
  2. Ask each supplier for current standard lead time, expedite options and how long today's quote stays valid, especially into first-half 2027.
  3. Flag any quote older than 30 days or any promise date that moved out more than two weeks, and decide which lines to firm with a blanket PO or alternate source.

Tip of the DayFor Material managers

Have AI rank your expedite list from open POs and shortages

Expedite calls eat the morning when every past-due line looks urgent. Export open purchase orders and the shortage or MRP exception list for one planner, anonymized, and have your approved AI tool score each line by days late, shortage risk and extended value. It drafts the call order; you still decide who gets a phone call and who gets an email.

You are a materials manager building today's expedite call list. Inputs, anonymized: (a) open POs, one row per line: PO number, item code, supplier code, open qty, promise date, unit price; (b) shortages or MRP exceptions, one row per item: item code, shortage qty, need date, customer or work-order code.
[PASTE OPEN POS]
[PASTE SHORTAGES]
Today's date: [DATE]
1) Join the two lists on item code. Flag every open PO line that covers a shortage or is already past the promise date.
2) Score each flagged line: days late (or days until need date if not late), shortage qty, and extended value (open qty times unit price).
3) Rank by a simple priority: shortages first, then days late, then extended value. List the top fifteen to work today.
4) Draft a two-sentence expedite note template with blanks for promise date and ship method.
Output: a table with columns priority, PO number, item code, supplier code, days late, shortage qty, extended value, suggested action; then the note template. Keep it under 300 words. Use anonymized data only. List any missing or inconsistent data instead of guessing.

2. Generac's data-center generator backlog hits about $4.5 billion after Amazon deal

About $4.5 billion. That's Generac's data-center generator backlog as of its October 28 results, nearly triple the level from 90 days earlier, after a long-term supply agreement with Amazon for backup power at data centers. Initial deliveries under that deal are expected to total a combined $2.4 billion in 2027 and 2028. C&I segment external sales rose about 28% in the quarter, and management said data-center demand is still adding orders from non-hyperscale customers too.

So what for buyersIf backup generators or related electrical packages sit on your 2027 plan, ask suppliers for slot dates and deposit terms now, not at kickoff.

3. Luminovo opens electronics BOMs and lead times to ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot

Ask your AI assistant for the EOL parts and 12-week lead times in your active BOMs. On November 5 Luminovo said it is opening its electronics supply-chain platform to assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot through the Model Context Protocol, with a beta for selected customers at electronica in Munich. An EMS cost estimator can price a customer BOM against current contracts; a buyer can ask which active-BOM parts are end-of-life. Luminovo says Omega EMS cut complex BOM quoting from about half a day to about 20 minutes.

So what for materials managersIf your BOM and lead-time data still live in spreadsheets and email, ask your approved AI tool what it can read today, and write down what still needs a human check.

4. Geek+ maps a 24/7 warehouse autonomy plan around embodied AI and a warehouse brain

Overnight shifts without adding overnight headcount. On November 6 Geek+ announced a 24/7 Warehouse Autonomy Plan that pairs embodied AI for harder picking and exception handling with its Warehouse Brain software (robot management, warehouse execution and operations intelligence). The goal is warehouses that keep running nights and weekends with less continuous on-site supervision, while learning from live operations. CEO Yong Zheng said the next goal is for the warehouse as a whole to operate more autonomously.

So what for warehouse managersIf you already run AMRs, ask your vendor which overnight exceptions still need a person on site, and what data would retire those exceptions.
