AI production scheduling: real AI examples and a starter prompt

AI builds and re-builds production schedules around orders, machine capacity, changeovers and material availability, so lines run fuller and orders ship on time.

Best for: Operations and plant managers, Planners

Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

I have [N] jobs (due date, hours, required machine) and [M] machines with [SHIFT] shifts. Write a simple scheduling approach (Excel Solver or Python OR-Tools) that minimizes late jobs, and explain how to add changeover times later.
