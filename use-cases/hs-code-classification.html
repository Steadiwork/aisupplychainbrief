Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

Suggest the most likely 6-digit HS heading for this product: [DESCRIPTION, MATERIAL, FUNCTION, USE]. Give your reasoning using the General Rules of Interpretation, list 2 alternative headings, and say what extra facts would settle it. I will verify with a licensed broker.
