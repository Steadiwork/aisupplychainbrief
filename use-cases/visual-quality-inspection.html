AI visual quality inspection: real AI examples and a starter prompt

Cameras plus AI models spot defects (weld spatter, paint flaws, cracks) on the line in near real time. At Audi, eight cameras check car underbodies for weld spatter on lines building about 1,000 cars a day.

Best for: Operations and plant managers

Real-world examples

Audi

Eight cameras and an AI model check car-body underbodies for weld spatter in near real time and guide operators with lights to remove it, on lines with about 2,000 welds per body and 1,000 cars a day.

Source: Siemens customer story

BMW

Uses AI in paint-shop surface inspection to detect and record tiny defects, supporting automated correction.

Source: Automotive Manufacturing Solutions
