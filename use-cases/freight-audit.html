AI freight audit and pay: real AI examples and a starter prompt

AI checks every carrier invoice against contracted rates, accessorials and shipment data, catching overcharges before payment.

Best for: Logistics leads, Supply chain managers

Real-world examples

Clemens Food Group

AI transportation audit prevented $1.2M in logistics costs, including $124,330 in overcharges across 22,054 shipments.

Source: Supply Chain 24/7

Tools

Free or low-cost options first, then enterprise platforms.

  • Excel + General-purpose AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot; free tiers) for rate-check formulas
  • Loop
  • Pando
  • Cass Information Systems
Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

Here are anonymized carrier invoice lines (lane, weight, base rate, fuel, accessorials) and my contracted rate table. Write Excel formulas that flag lines where charges exceed contract by more than [X]%, and group flagged amounts by carrier and charge type.
