Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

Here is 24 months of anonymized monthly demand for [N] SKUs plus a promo flag column. Suggest a forecasting approach, write the Excel or Python steps to produce a 6-month forecast, and tell me how to measure accuracy (MAPE and bias) against a naive forecast.
