Autonomous mobile robots navigate the warehouse with AI and bring work to pickers, raising picks per hour and cutting walking time.

Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

Here is anonymized pick data (SKU, location, picks per week). Suggest a slotting change that moves the fastest movers closest to pack-out, estimate the walking distance saved, and list what to check before considering robots.
