Predictive maintenance: real AI examples and a starter prompt

AI watches machine data (vibration, temperature, current) to predict failures weeks ahead, so maintenance and spare parts are planned instead of rushed.

Best for: Operations and plant managers

Real-world examples

Nissan

Monitors 9,000 connected assets across multiple sites, getting 2 weeks to 6 months of warning before failures, with ROI in under 3 months.

Source: Senseye case study (via CaseStudies.com)

Aluminium smelter in East Iceland

Reduced unplanned downtime by 20% using existing data, with ROI in 4 to 6 months.

Source: Siemens case study (PDF)

Free or low-cost options first, then enterprise platforms.

Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

Here is an anonymized export of daily readings for one machine (vibration, temperature, run hours) plus dates of past failures. Suggest simple warning thresholds or a trend rule that would have flagged those failures early, and how to test it without over-alerting.
