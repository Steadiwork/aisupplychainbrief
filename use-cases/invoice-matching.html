Invoice capture and PO matching: real AI examples and a starter prompt

AI reads invoices in any layout, extracts the data, and matches it to purchase orders and receipts, so only exceptions need a person.

Best for: Buyers, Procurement leaders

Real-world examples

Fugro

Cut average invoice processing time from 2 minutes to 35 seconds with a 70% automation rate across 21 countries.

Maximus

Source: Rossum customer story

Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

Write an Excel formula approach that compares an invoice list (columns: PO#, line, qty, unit price) with a PO list and a receipts list, and flags lines where qty or price differ by more than [TOLERANCE]%. Explain each formula in one line.
