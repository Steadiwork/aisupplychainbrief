Inventory optimization: real AI examples and a starter prompt

AI sets safety stock and reorder points per item based on demand variability and target service levels, cutting excess stock without more stockouts.

Best for: Planners, Materials managers

Real-world examples

Gaviota

Cut stock from 61 to 35 days of coverage while raising service levels on top items from 70% to 95%.

Source: ToolsGroup news release

Aston Martin

Reduced safety-stock value 18% while lifting service to 97.1%.

Source: ToolsGroup customer story

Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

For these anonymized items (avg weekly demand, std dev, lead time in weeks, unit cost), calculate safety stock and reorder point at a [95]% service level. Show the formula, then list the 5 items where inventory dollars could drop most. Show your math.
