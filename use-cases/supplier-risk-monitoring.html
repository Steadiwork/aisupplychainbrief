Supplier risk monitoring: real AI examples and a starter prompt

AI scans news, sanctions lists, financial signals and other sources across your supplier base and alerts you to events (fires, strikes, insolvency, compliance issues) before they hit your plants.

Best for: Procurement leaders, Supply chain managers

Real-world examples

Maersk

Supplier risk coverage grew from 2,000 to 60,000 suppliers within months, and tenders now receive risk insights in about a week.

Source: Responsibly case study

Kärcher

Monitors more than 83,000 Tier-N suppliers, cutting risk-management effort 40x and reacting to risks 3 days faster.

Source: Prewave case study (via CaseStudies.com)

Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

Here are recent news headlines and notes about a supplier that makes [PART TYPE] in [REGION] (name removed). Rate the supply risk as low/medium/high for the next 90 days, list the specific signals behind your rating, and suggest 3 mitigation steps. Say what you are unsure about.
