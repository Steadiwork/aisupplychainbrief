Starter prompt

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Remove names and confidential numbers first.

Draft an RFQ for [ITEM/SERVICE] with these specs: [SPECS], annual volume [VOLUME], delivery to [REGION], due date [DATE]. Include a bid-sheet table with the fields suppliers must fill in (unit price, MOQ, lead time, payment terms, Incoterms) and 5 clarifying questions suppliers usually ask.
