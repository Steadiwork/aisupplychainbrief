Privacy

If you subscribe, your email address is used only to send you the AI Supply Chain Brief. Delivery is handled by follow.it, an RSS-to-email service, which stores your address to send the emails. Its own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops the emails right away.
