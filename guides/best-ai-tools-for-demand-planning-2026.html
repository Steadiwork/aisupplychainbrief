Best AI tools for demand planning (2026)

"AI demand planning" now covers everything from large planning suites to a spreadsheet plus a chatbot. This guide sorts the main options by company size and what they're good at, so you can build a shortlist. It's based on public vendor information. We have no paid placements, and you should always run a pilot on your own data. If you want to use a general AI assistant in your own planning work instead, see ChatGPT for demand planning.

What AI actually adds to demand planning

  • Machine-learning forecasts that pick up many drivers at once (price, promotions, seasonality, weather) instead of one statistical model per item.
  • Demand sensing: short-horizon updates from recent orders or point-of-sale data.
  • Exception management: flagging the few items that need a planner's attention.
  • Generative AI assistants that explain forecast changes in plain language and answer "what if" questions.

Enterprise planning suites

These suit large, multi-site companies running an integrated S&OP process. They're powerful, but implementations take months and need ERP integration.

  • o9 Solutions: a knowledge-graph-based planning platform covering demand, supply and IBP.
  • Kinaxis Maestro: known for concurrent planning and fast scenario analysis.
  • Blue Yonder: ML demand planning with deep retail and manufacturing coverage.
  • SAP IBP: a natural fit if you already run SAP.
  • Oracle Supply Chain Planning: a natural fit for Oracle Cloud ERP shops.

Retail and specialist forecasting

  • RELEX: retail and CPG forecasting and replenishment.
  • ToolsGroup: probabilistic forecasting and inventory optimization.
  • Lokad: a probabilistic, programmable approach for teams that have data skills.

Mid-market and small business

  • Netstock: forecasting and inventory planning that sits on top of common small and mid-size business ERPs.
  • inFlow and Zoho Inventory: inventory tools with basic reorder planning, good for small teams.

Do-it-yourself and low-cost options

  • Excel's Forecast Sheet / FORECAST.ETS: exponential smoothing, already in most offices.
  • Open-source Python libraries such as statsforecast, Prophet and Darts, for teams that have an analyst.
  • General AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot) to review a forecast, explain error, or draft S&OP commentary. Use anonymized data and your company's approved tools only.

How to choose: a 6-question checklist

  1. How many SKU-locations do you plan, and how often?
  2. Which ERP must it integrate with?
  3. Do you need demand sensing (daily or weekly) or monthly consensus planning?
  4. Will the vendor run a pilot on your history and report accuracy (MAPE and bias) against your current method?
  5. Can planners see why the forecast changed (explainability)?
  6. What's the total cost: licenses, implementation and internal time?
