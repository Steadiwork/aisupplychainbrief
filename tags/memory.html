Samsung says its 2027 HBM supply is sold out and memory stays tight through 2028

Sold out for 2027, and customers still want more. Samsung said on its third-quarter call on October 29 that its 2027 high-bandwidth memory supply is fully booked at its target prices, and that it expects tight memory supply to last through 2028. The company said new fabs and equipment take too long to lift output soon, and that bigger HBM chips eat into capacity for regular DRAM.

SK hynix says its DRAM and HBM output is effectively sold out through 2029

Sold out through 2029. SK hynix said on its third-quarter earnings call on October 28 that, counting long-term agreements and deals under discussion, its DRAM and high-bandwidth memory output for the next three years is effectively spoken for, and it expects memory demand to outrun supply through 2030. The company said customer requests for HBM are more than twice what it can supply, and that long-term agreements already cover more than half its capacity for 2027 and 2028. It also expects PC and phone shipments to fall as memory costs rise.

Micron's Taiwan union plans a Thursday strike at an AI memory hub

A strike set for Thursday, October 22, at Micron's plant in Taoyuan, Taiwan, where the company makes DRAM and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) for AI servers. The Taoyuan union announced it on October 19 to back a second union in a bonus dispute, timing it to that union's mediation meeting with Micron the same day, Reuters reported. The union hasn't said how long the walkout will last, and TrendForce says any hit to output depends on its size, its length and Micron's contingency plans.

So whatCheck which memory parts trace to Micron Taiwan and get lead-time status in writing before anyone panic-buys.

AI's appetite for memory just caused the worst quarter on record for smartphones

IDC's preliminary numbers show global smartphone shipments fell 22% year over year in the third quarter to about 255 million units, the steepest quarterly drop it has ever recorded. IDC blames the AI-driven memory shortage: memory costs are up roughly 350% from a year ago, and vendors have cut low-end models and raised prices. IDC expects pressure to last through 2027 and warns that some makers built ahead of next year's expected component squeeze, which could leave excess inventory if demand stays soft.

So whatIf any of your products use DRAM or flash, treat memory as a constrained, fast-moving cost input in your plan.
