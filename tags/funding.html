Opply raises $7.5M for AI agents that place and pay purchase orders

15 times the gross revenue in 12 months. That's the growth Opply reported on October 21 as it raised $7.5 million in a round led by Chalfen Ventures. The London company's agents find suppliers, place and track purchase orders, check compliance documents and pay suppliers for food and drink brands, which repay Opply within 90 days, Tech Funding News reported. Opply cites customer cost cuts of 40%; Tech Funding News points out that part of that benefit comes from longer payment terms Opply extends, not only from better prices.

An AI operator is auditing every LTL invoice at one freight broker

Nearly 3,500 audit decisions in two months, at 97% accuracy by the vendor's own count. That's what startup Evos reports for the AI operator that freight brokerage MyFreightWorld uses to check every LTL carrier invoice against its TMS; Evos says it closed out more than $600,000 of invoice value in the last five weeks. Evos came out of stealth on October 14 with $9 million in pre-seed funding, and it hasn't disclosed pricing or how much money the audits actually recovered.

Read the story →

So whatFreight audit suits AI because every call can be checked against a rate. Track dollars recovered, not invoices processed.

Ultra raises $50M to lease packing robots to warehouses by the month

Brooklyn-based Ultra closed a $50M Series A led by Framework Ventures, bringing total funding to $62M. It installs its OP1 packing robots at warehouses and third-party logistics sites for an upfront integration fee plus a monthly service fee, rather than a big capital purchase. The company says its robots have packed over 500,000 orders, and it relies on Physical Intelligence for the AI models that run them.

Scimplify raises $25M to add US capacity for specialty and critical-materials chemistry

Scimplify, a Bengaluru-based specialty chemicals and advanced materials maker, raised $25M in a round led by Hitachi Ventures, with Henkel and Teknor Apex joining. Total funding is now about $80M. It plans US research and processing capacity for areas with thin domestic supply, such as rare-earth and critical-metal purification and high-purity semiconductor and battery chemicals, and will keep building its in-house AI platform for taking chemistry from lab to production.

Broadcom is lining up $30–50B+ to finance OpenAI's custom chips

The WSJ reported that Broadcom is working to arrange more than $50B in financing for the custom AI chips it is co-developing with OpenAI, with Apollo and Blackstone in talks. Bloomberg put the figure nearer $30B and called the talks early. Either way, private credit is now funding chip supply directly.
