Foundry Robotics raises $100 million to run AI-driven assembly cells as a service

Twenty times more floor space. Foundry Robotics has moved into a 130,000-square-foot site in Emeryville, California, and on October 28 announced a $100 million Series A led by Marlinspike Partners. The company owns and runs the factory, using AI-trained robotic cells to assemble complex electro-mechanical products such as drones, wire harnesses, satellites and telecom and data center gear. Foundry says customers go from design to production in weeks without building their own lines.

A robot-run U.S. factory says it builds circuit boards in 4 days, at China prices

Four days, not nine. That's the average build-and-ship time CircuitHub says its automated circuit board factory in South Deerfield, Massachusetts, now hits, compared with a typical quick-turn shop in China. The company announced on October 26 that the factory, called the Grid, has reached price parity with similar Chinese shops and is expanding capacity fivefold, with a UK site planned for 2027. Levitating movers carry each board between robots that print solder paste, place parts, solder and inspect, and AI routes every board on its own path, so there are no changeovers between designs. CircuitHub says one unit each of 100 designs runs as easily as 100 units of one design, and that it has shipped more than 2.2 million boards. Why it matters: CircuitHub says 95% of electronics projects involve fewer than 10,000 units, and those small, high-mix orders are where offshore lead time and freight hurt most. Price parity is the company's own claim, so test it with a quote.

So whatRe-quote one low-volume board assembly domestically and compare landed cost and total lead time.

NVIDIA's DSX POD ships AI data centers as factory-built containers

Six months down to as little as 14 days. That's NVIDIA's estimate for bringing AI computing online at a prepared site with DSX POD, a design it introduced on October 21 in which partners build complete data-center modules inside containers. Each holds seven Vera Rubin NVL72 racks plus networking, cabling and liquid cooling, all tested before shipping, Data Center Knowledge reported. NVIDIA names 17 partners, including Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Schneider Electric and Vertiv, with deliveries from early 2027. NVIDIA's own FAQ is clear that sites still need power, cooling water and network hookups ready first.

So whatThe 14 days starts only when site power and cooling are ready. Buy those first; get delivery slots in writing.

Siemens puts an AI agent to work on production planning at its Erlangen plant

Up to 20% lower costs from an AI agent that drafts production plans: that's Siemens' own estimate from its electronics factory in Erlangen, Germany, where it showed new tools built with NVIDIA on October 19. When a new order arrives, the agent generates several production scenarios, shows planners how each one scores on defined KPIs, and checks the proposals against a digital twin of the whole plant before anything changes on the floor. A second tool trains the plant's inspection cameras on AI-generated images of new circuit boards, so quality checks can be set up before the first prototype exists; Siemens says that cuts engineering effort by up to 85% and time to market by up to 40%. German tech site Dr. Web notes these are best-case figures from Siemens and haven't been independently checked. Why it matters: a planning agent can only rank options against goals someone has written down, such as on-time delivery, changeovers, overtime and material shortages. Plants that haven't agreed on those will get fast answers to the wrong question.

Adani and Jabil sign up to build AI racks and servers in India

Adani Enterprises and contract manufacturer Jabil signed a non-binding memorandum of understanding on October 13 for a 50:50 joint venture to make and assemble AI racks, servers, storage, networking gear, power distribution, liquid cooling and transformers in India, plus installation and commissioning. The companies are aiming for multi-gigawatt rack capacity for Indian and export customers. No investment figure or production date was given, and the deal still needs definitive agreements and approvals.

TSMC: AI chip demand is still bigger than its capacity, and it's scouting Texas

TSMC reported third-quarter revenue of about $46.7B, up roughly 51% from a year ago, and guided fourth-quarter revenue to $52.3B to $54.3B. Management said AI-related demand remains very strong and the gap between demand and supply is still large, with 2026 capital spending planned at $60B to $64B. The WSJ reported that TSMC has sent teams to Texas to evaluate expansion beyond Arizona.

Physical Intelligence opens its robot models to outside developers

Physical Intelligence launched a waitlisted beta of its Pi API on October 13. Teams upload their own robot data, fine-tune the company's general-purpose models on its managed infrastructure, and run them through a hosted service. Robots-as-a-service provider Formic, which says 150 factories use its robots, is an early partner. The company noted that network connectivity was a challenge in some early cloud-run deployments.
